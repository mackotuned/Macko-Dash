MACKODASH USB UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS
================================

Included files:
  MackoDashUpdateFlasher.exe
  MackoDash-Firmware.zip

You may keep both files together for automatic bundle detection, or select a
newer official MackoDash-Firmware.zip downloaded from GitHub.

1. Connect MackoDash to the Windows PC with its update USB cable.
2. Keep the dashboard powered for the entire update.
3. Open MackoDashUpdateFlasher.exe.
4. Select the official full firmware ZIP and confirm it shows as Validated.
5. Confirm the dashboard COM port. The app normally selects it automatically.
6. Select Flash ESP32-P4 Firmware.
7. Confirm the warning and wait for flash verification to finish.
8. Do not unplug USB or power until Update Complete appears.

This replaces the ESP32-P4 bootloader, partition table, OTA metadata,
application, and onboard SPIFFS. It preserves NVS settings, odometer data,
SD-card themes, and ESP32-C6 firmware.

If the USB port is busy, close any serial monitor or other flashing program and
try again. If an update fails, leave USB and power connected, review the error,
and retry. Do not use Erase Flash or another firmware file.
